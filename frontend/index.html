            How a check works

            1. Understand. The AI picks out the main claim — even from a long forward or a link.

            2. Search. It looks up live news, published fact-checks and background facts from Wikipedia.

            3. Weigh. It compares the claim with that evidence, notes warning signs, and shows you the sources.

            It can still be wrong: brand-new events may not be reported yet, sources can disagree, and AI sometimes misreads things. Treat the result as a starting point, not the final word.
